Self-hosted web fonts for AG Nova Slate Hub (Latin subset).

  Space Grotesk   variable, wght 300-700   (headings)
  Inter Tight     variable, wght 100-900   (body copy)
  IBM Plex Mono   400 / 500 / 600 / 700    (data)

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The .woff files are legacy-format copies of the
IBM Plex Mono 400 / 700 faces, used only by the Kindle dashboard (old WebKit).
